## 4. Key Actions Needed to Upload to Arthur Online
Once the client provides the missing documents, the following actions must be taken in Arthur Online to ensure they sync successfully:
1. **Tenant Contact Details:** Enter the email address for each tenant in the Arthur Tenancy profile. This allows our system to link their login account to their flat.
2. **Compliance Certificates (EPC / EICR):** Upload these documents under the *Documents* tab of the corresponding Unit. Ensure they are tagged as "EPC" or "EICR" respectively.
3. **Deposit Status:** Update the tenancy status in Arthur to indicate that the deposit is "Received" and "Lodged with DPS" once proof is obtained.
4. **Tenancy Agreements:** Ensure all tenancy agreements are marked as "Signed" and the PDF copy is attached to the tenancy record.
